# Roadmap template

Aim for roughly 2,500-4,500 words; tasks and epics are tables. Sections in this order; drop one only if irrelevant and say so in one line. No code, no re-design of earlier steps.

````markdown
# <Name> — Roadmap

> **Links:** [concept](concept.md) · [requirements](requirements.md) · [resource-math](resource-math.md) · [contracts](contracts.md) · [data](data.md) · [architecture](architecture.md) · [mechanics](mechanics.md) · [resilience](resilience.md) · [decisions](decisions.md)
> **Status:** draft / agreed (agreed only when the clarity gate passes, SKILL.md step 6) · **Date:** <date> · **Based on:** requirements.md (<date>), resource-math.md (<date>), contracts.md (<date>), data.md (<date>), architecture.md (<date>), mechanics.md (<date>), resilience.md (<date>)
> **Counts:** <n> PH · <n> G · <n> TK · <n> EP   (the checker compares these with the tables)

## 1. Summary
The plan in ≤5 sentences: how many phases and gates, what phase 0 must prove, the thin slice, the calendar verdict in the three scenarios, the cuts that make it fit, the first thing to do.

## 2. Capacity and deadline
| Scenario | Hours per week | Deadline (weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| best | 14 | 40 |
| expected | 10 | 40 |
| worst | 6 | 40 |

Where the numbers come from (the developer's statement, a requirement, an assumption). A deadline may refer to the end of one phase - write it as `52 @PH-3` - otherwise it applies to the whole plan.

## 3. Phases
| ID | Phase | Goal | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| PH-0 | Prove the risks | spikes, measurements, drills that can kill or reshape the project | tasks |
| PH-1 | Thin slice | one scenario end to end through the real structure | tasks |
| PH-2 | Increment: … | … | epic |

## 4. Gates
| ID | Phase | Criteria (measurable) | If fail (continue / change / stop) |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-0 | PH-0 | cost per goal ≤ $20 (L-1, NFR-1); DR-1 and DR-4 passed; micro-VM resumes in ≤ 15 s | continue / change the design: managed sandbox / stop |

## 5. Tasks (detailed, up to the nearest gates)
| ID | Task | Phase | Best | Expected | Worst | Depends | Wait (weeks) | Source | Serves | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TK-1 | Measure cost per goal on 10 real goals | PH-0 | 8 | 12 | 20 | — | 0 | spike P-1, RA-7 | NFR-1, L-1 | cost per goal measured; the expected scenario of resource-math updated |

`Source`: where the task comes from (a `→ roadmap` line, `DR-n`, `MC-n`, `D-n`, a failing limit, a requirement). `Serves`: requirement, limit or drill ids. `Acceptance`: how anyone can check it is done.

## 6. Epics (later phases, hour ranges only)
| ID | Epic | Phase | Best | Expected | Worst | Depends | Wait (weeks) | Source | Serves | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EP-1 | Approvals and delegation rules | PH-2 | 40 | 70 | 120 | TK-9 | 0 | FR-17…FR-20, MC-9 | FR-17…FR-20 | detailed after G-1 |

## 8. Cuts and options
If the expected scenario misses the deadline: what to cut (Should items, hours saved), what to shrink, what capacity or date would fit, the recommendation and its `D-<n>`.

## 9. Risks of the plan
Ranked: estimates with the widest ranges, external waits, dependency on a single spike, capacity assumptions. Each with the cheapest way to reduce it.

## 10. Reconciliation with earlier steps
What planning changed or found in earlier documents (a requirement that cannot fit, a missing step, a drill without an owner) with the decision or the pending question.

## 11. Deferred
One line per detail for re-planning after a gate.

## 12. Consistency check
Date; `check_roadmap.py` result (errors / warnings left and why); verdict of the independent reviewer; what was fixed; what stays open.

## Sources
Numbered URLs; delegated findings link to `[R-n]` reports with their confidence.
````
